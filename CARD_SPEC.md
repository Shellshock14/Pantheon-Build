# Pantheon frame specifications

- Card: 2.5 × 3.5 inches (5:7 aspect ratio)
- Export: 1500 × 2100 PNG (600 pixels per inch)
- Frame colors: blue/Champions, rust orange/Relics, purple/Divine Sites, red/Monsters, gold/Divine Blessings, black/typeless, teal/Blessings, white/Mortal.
- Every color has ivory and black rules-text-panel variants.
- Blank top banner is reserved for the card name; center banner for type; lower panel for rules.
- Black is typeless and may be titled Curse of Mortality on individual cards.
- White starting cards: Fate (+1 Favor, cost 0, VP 0) and Misfortune (blank rules, cost 0, VP 1).
- The art window is transparent in the PNGs.

## Asset limitation
These are preview-derived raster assets, upscaled from concept-sheet artwork and masked for transparency. Although delivered at 1500 × 2100, they are **not native 600-DPI illustrations**. The crop/mask and type/banner positions are approximate and should be checked before professional printing.
